What happens to your files
  • They never leave this device. Nothing is uploaded. We cannot see, store or share your files, and neither can the person you are checking.
  • They are gone when you close or refresh this page. Keep your own copies. You will need them for a report.
  • A file changes the score only if you ask. Tap "Read text" on a screenshot and its words are read here, on this device, then added to step 4 where you can see and correct them. Reading works for English text and can make mistakes. PDFs are not read.
  • Adding files alone does nothing to the score. Nobody can raise a score against a person by piling on unrelated files.
  • Your files are for you. They are listed in your evidence summary so you can attach them when you report.

    The summary lists the profile, your result, your note and your file names, ready to paste into a report. It contains file names only, never the files themselves.

    Five rules that stop most scams

    This page is free for everyone and needs no sign-up.

    1. Slow down. Anyone who says "now or never" is rushing you on purpose.
    2. Codes are keys. Never share an OTP, password or PIN, even with a friend's account that asks.
    3. Money to strangers does not come back. Gift cards, crypto and instant transfers are almost impossible to reverse.
    4. Check outside the chat. Call the person, the bank or the company on a number you find yourself.
    5. Tell someone. Scams depend on secrecy. Saying it out loud to one trusted person usually breaks the spell.
